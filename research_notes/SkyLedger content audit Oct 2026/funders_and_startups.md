## Q1. New funding rounds in 2026 for SRM / weather-modification startups

### Takeaway
The only large 2026 round in this space is Rainmaker Technology's $100M Series B (announced Sept 21, 2026; lead not disclosed; Lowercarbon Capital participated). ## Q2. Updated total private funding into SRM startups (citable figure)

### Takeaway
The "$115.8M" figure comes from E&E News (Dec 23, 2025): nine startups, 50+ funders, with Stardust ~65% of it. No source has published a 2026 update of that startup-only total. The most-cited 2026 figures are from the Böll/Non-Use Agreement study ("Who Funds and Who Pays," March 2026): ~$60M in 2025 across all SRM funding, ~$120M if Stardust's $60M is added — but that is all-sources (philanthropy + government + VC), not startups only.

### Cited Findings
- E&E News, Dec 23, 2025 (Corbin Hiar): "more than $115.8 million from 50+ funders to nine startups." Breakdown: Stardust ~$75M (~65%); Reflect Orbital ~$28.7M (Sequoia, Lux); Gama (French) ~$5M since 2020; Arctic Reflections (Dutch) $4.41M from ARIA; Make Sunsets (Draper-funded, amount not given). ARIA says it takes no equity. Named investors: Finn Murphy (Nebular) >$1M in Stardust; Evan Caron (Montauk Capital); Lowercarbon (partner Ryan Orbuch quoted); Exor; Sequoia; Draper Associates; Lux; Possible Ventures (Gama). — [E&E News, Dec 23, 2025 (background)](https://www.eenews.net/articles/betting-on-climate-failure-these-investors-could-earn-billions/)
- "Who Funds and Who Pays? The funding of solar geoengineering, 2020–2025" (Jared Sanborn and J.P. Sapinski; Heinrich Böll Foundation report dated March 9, 2026 in URL; blog summary May 12, 2026): database of 43 funders and 166 recipients; known funding rose from <$6M (2020) to $30M (2024) to $60M (2025) in constant 2024 dollars; adding Stardust's $60M makes 2025 ~$120M. Data collection ended Sept 2025. Funder share by count: philanthropy >50%, private investors/VC ~20%, governments <12%. Named funders by recipient count: LAD Climate Fund 16, Quadrature 8, Coefficient Giving (formerly Open Philanthropy) 5, Outlier Projects 4, Gigascale Capital 3, Astera Institute 3, Amazon 2, Gates Foundation 2, Lowercarbon 1, Simons "multiple." — [solargeoeng.org, May 12, 2026](https://www.solargeoeng.org/who-funds-and-who-pays-the-funding-of-solar-geoengineering-2020-2025/); [Böll PDF](https://www.boell.de/sites/default/files/2026-04/boll.paper_who_funds_and_who-pays_barrierefrei.pdf); [HOME Alliance key highlights, March 2026](https://handsoffmotherearth.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Key-Highlights-from-research-report-%E2%80%98Who-Funds-and-Who-Pays_-the-funding-of-solar-geoengineering.pdf)
- Geoengineering Monitor (Anja Chalmin, Feb 23, 2026): SRM funding "nearly tripled in 2025 vs 2024," mostly public money. 2025 by funder (EUR): ARIA €62.48M, Stardust €50.4M, NERC €11.55M, Swiss NSF €2.28M; Quadrature €36.9M over three years. Three organizations (ARIA, Quadrature, Simons) have provided or pledged ~$150M since 2024. — [Geoengineering Monitor, Feb 23, 2026](https://www.geoengineeringmonitor.org/geoengineering-the-stratosphere)
- Medscape (2026) secondary summary: ~$120M known funding in 2025; private sources >70% of total; governments <12%. — [Medscape, 2026](https://www.medscape.com/viewarticle/solar-geoengineering-climate-cure-or-health-gamble-2026a1000skt)
- No SRM360, Heatmap, Carbon Brief, PitchBook, or CTVC article publishing a 2026 startup-only total surfaced in searches. Heatmap's latest relevant pieces are 2025 ([Heatmap "Wackiest Climate Tech Bets of 2025"](https://heatmap.news/climate-tech/wackiest-climate-tech-investments-2025)).

### Inferences
- An honest 2026 update of SkyLedger's "$115.8M" line: "$115.8M (E&E, Dec 2025) for nine SRM/sunlight-reflection startups; no new SRM startup equity disclosed since, except Rainmaker's $100M Series B (Sept 2026) in cloud seeding." If cloud seeding is included, "roughly $216M+" is arithmetically defensible but not a published figure — label it SkyLedger's own sum.
- The "$120M in 2025" Böll figure is frequently misquoted as "startup funding"; it is all-sources and excludes ARIA/Quadrature multi-year totals counted elsewhere. Avoid mixing it with the E&E startup total.

### Gaps
- No citable source gives a 2026 cumulative private-startup total. The report writer should present E&E's $115.8M as of Dec 2025 plus Rainmaker separately.
- Gama's and Arctic Reflections' 2026 status not checked.
