## Key Question 1: H.R. 4403 Clear Skies Act — current status, cosponsors, committee action, Senate companion, floor vote, effect of Greene's resignation

### Takeaway
H.R. 4403 has had zero actions since its July 15, 2025 referral to House Energy and Commerce; it still has exactly 3 cosponsors (the last, Massie, joined Nov 4, 2025), no markup, no hearing on the bill itself, no floor vote, and no Senate companion. ## Key Question 5: GAO report on NOAA weather modification reporting — exact title, number, date, findings, follow-up

### Takeaway
The report is GAO-26-108013, "Weather Modification: NOAA Should Strengthen Oversight to Ensure Reliable Information," published and publicly released February 23, 2026, requested by Rep. Robert Garcia (Ranking Member, House Oversight). The site's "78%" figure is correct but mislabeled (it is the estimated share of *initial* reports with at least one error; overall "over half" of all reports), the "10 states had unreported activities" claim is wrong (GAO found 4 of 10 states reviewed had activities missing from NOAA's database), and "NOAA has never issued a penalty" is correct.

### Cited Findings
- CONFIRMED (GAO): Title "Weather Modification: NOAA Should Strengthen Oversight to Ensure Reliable Information," GAO-26-108013, published and publicly released February 23, 2026; three recommendations (one to Commerce, two to NOAA); NOAA agreed with all three; all three "Open" as of Oct 26, 2026. — [GAO product page](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-26-108013); [full report HTML](https://files.gao.gov/reports/GAO-26-108013/index.html); [PDF](https://www.gao.gov/assets/gao-26-108013.pdf)
- CONFIRMED (GAO full report): congressional requester = Robert Garcia, Ranking Member, House Committee on Oversight and Government Reform. — [GAO full report](https://files.gao.gov/reports/GAO-26-108013/index.html)
- CONFIRMED (GAO full report): NOAA's public database held 1,084 files as of February 2025; GAO reviewed a random sample of 111 files (169 individual initial/interim/final reports). — [GAO full report](https://files.gao.gov/reports/GAO-26-108013/index.html)
- CONFIRMED (GAO full report): an estimated 78% of *initial* reports have one or more errors (95% CI 68–86%); an estimated 21% of interim and final reports have one or more errors (95% CI 13–31%); GAO's headline: "over half of all the reports filed with NOAA likely have errors, including missing required information." — [GAO full report](https://files.gao.gov/reports/GAO-26-108013/index.html); [GAO product page](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-26-108013)
- CONFIRMED (GAO full report): an estimated 32% of initial reports are missing a required map; an estimated 36% include a map missing one or more required components. — [GAO full report](https://files.gao.gov/reports/GAO-26-108013/index.html)
- CONFIRMED (GAO full report): of 10 states whose records GAO compared (2021–2025), 4 had reported weather modification activities that were missing from NOAA's database. — [GAO full report](https://files.gao.gov/reports/GAO-26-108013/index.html)
- CONFIRMED (GAO full report): NOAA officials said the agency has never imposed a fine under the Act; the statutory $10,000 maximum (1971) would be over $60,000 inflation-adjusted. — [GAO full report](https://files.gao.gov/reports/GAO-26-108013/index.html)
- CONFIRMED (GAO full report): only 4 of 1,084 files concern solar geoengineering; NOAA's forms "have remained virtually unchanged since 1974"; NOAA has no dedicated funding for weather modification oversight. — [GAO full report](https://files.gao.gov/reports/GAO-26-108013/index.html); [GAO PDF](https://www.gao.gov/assets/gao-26-108013.pdf)
- CONFIRMED (GAO full report): in 2024, state and local agencies in nine states spent or planned to spend at least $20 million combined on cloud seeding; Utah typically budgets $5 million/year; an Oregon irrigation district got $76,000 in federal funds in 2024 to investigate cloud seeding. — [GAO full report](https://files.gao.gov/reports/GAO-26-108013/index.html); [E&E News, 2026-02-23](https://www.eenews.net/articles/noaa-must-ramp-up-oversight-of-weather-modification-gao-says/)
- CONFIRMED (GAO): Recommendations (verbatim via House Science letter quoting GAO): "(1) establish written agency guidance for its review of and confirmation that all weather modification reports submitted to NOAA contain the legally required information; (2) improve NOAA's instructions on how to complete the reporting forms for a range of weather modification activities, including emerging solar geoengineering activities; and (3) implement a process to regularly inform the operators as well as state and local agencies directly about NOAA's role in weather modification oversight, the applicability of the Act to different activities, and where to find required reporting forms." — [House Science letter, 2026-05-22](https://republicans-science.house.gov/_cache/files/0/9/0933efe7-129f-4e61-a239-f50871516c1f/177E45E85678A21A928DA3AE95F5893AB1E0A1558E9431EF5251B22DDBE46247.05.22.26-iosst-to-noaa-cloud-brightening-.pdf); [GAO product page](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-26-108013)
- CONFIRMED (GAO): companion "Science & Tech Spotlight: Solar Geoengineering," GAO-26-108837, published March 24, 2026 (note: the House Science letter mis-dates it Feb 23, 2026); notes limited US federal oversight, some state bans, largely negative US public opinion, and that two startups have private funding including one at $75 million in 2025. — [GAO-26-108837](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-26-108837)
- REPORTED (NPR/CPR, 2026-04-07/08): GAO's Cardell Johnson: "the vast majority of them had missing information, incorrect information... a lot of reports were missing that should have been in there." — [CPR](https://www.cpr.org/2026/04/08/gao-wants-improved-weather-modification-data-noaa/); [KNPR/NPR](https://knpr.org/2026-04-07/the-gao-says-better-data-should-be-kept-about-weather-modification-efforts)
- CONFIRMED: Follow-up = May 22, 2026 House Science briefing request (KQ4). No NOAA implementation announced; no legislation responding to GAO introduced.

### Inferences
- Site copy should read roughly: "A February 23, 2026 GAO report (GAO-26-108013) estimated that 78% of initial weather-modification reports filed with NOAA contain at least one error, that over half of all reports likely have errors, that 4 of 10 states GAO checked had activities missing from NOAA's database, and that NOAA has never issued a fine in the Act's 50+ years."

### Gaps
