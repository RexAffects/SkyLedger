# US State Weather-Modification / Geoengineering Legislation — Status as of 2026-10-26

Scope: every US state law or bill to ban/restrict weather modification, geoengineering, "chemtrails," solar radiation management (SRM/SAI), or cloud seeding. Purpose: correct the claims on skyledger.org/learn/state-bans (last updated ~Mar–Apr 2026). Research date: 2026-10-26. Most recent evidence date is noted per claim.

Headline corrections for the site (details and sources in the sections below):
1. "3 states have enacted bans" is still correct in the sense of *three states with a standalone ban statute* (TN, FL, LA) — BUT Louisiana enacted a **second** law in 2026 (SB 189 → Act No. 601, effective Aug 1, 2026) that amends its 2025 ban. No fourth state has enacted a ban. Arizona passed one through both chambers and it was **vetoed** (March 13, 2026).
2. Every bill the site lists as "pending" has either died or rolled to a successor bill. Iowa did NOT pass its ban (both HF 2640 and SF 2208 died at the second funnel; the 2026 session is over). Oklahoma SB 430 died. New Hampshire HB 1618 was killed Feb 5, 2026. Rhode Island H 5217 died in 2025 and its 2026 successor H 7422 was "held for further study." New Jersey S4161 died and was reintroduced as S934 / A5246. Texas SB 1154 died in committee in 2025 (no 2026 regular session). Vermont H.217 died. Pennsylvania SB 508 is the only one of the site's "pending" list that is still technically alive (PA session runs to Nov 30, 2026), but it has had no action since March 21, 2025.
3. "30+ states have introduced bills" — defensible; best current trackers say 37 states (cumulative, SRM360 map as of July 27, 2026) and 31 states in the 2026 session alone (GeoLawWatch, updated Oct 20, 2026). "22 states introduced bills in 2025" matches SRM360's April 2025 count but SRM360 later revised the 2025 figure to 31 bills; use "22 states by April 2025" or cite the newer numbers.
4. Montana SB 473 did fail (House floor vote 45–55 on April 10, 2025). Several aggregator sites wrongly say Montana and Texas "passed" narrower bans — they did not.
5. Louisiana's bill number on the site should read **SB 46 (Act No. 95 of 2025)** — the site currently gives only "Act No. 95," which is fine, but the signing date (June 8, 2025) and effective date (Aug 1, 2025) should be added.
6. Site's "failed/died in committee" list is correct for 2025; it should be extended with the 2026 deaths listed below.
7. Cloud seeding: 9 active states still correct (GAO count), no state halted a program in 2026 per any source found; Texas's July 2025 pause was lifted.
8. No lawsuit challenging any enacted state ban was found.

## Key Question 1: Which states have ENACTED a ban or restriction as of Oct 26, 2026?

### Takeaway
Three states have enacted standalone prohibitions: Tennessee (2024), Florida (2025), Louisiana (2025). The only 2026 enactment anywhere is Louisiana's **Act No. 601 (SB 189)**, which amends the 2025 ban to explicitly cover dispersal "by the burning of fuel in an aircraft engine"; it became law without the governor's signature on June 1, 2026 and takes effect Aug 1, 2026. No other state enacted a ban in 2026; Arizona's bill was vetoed.

### Cited Findings

**Tennessee — SB 2691 / HB 2063 → Public Chapter 709 (2024)**
- Senate passed as amended 25–6 on 03/18/2024; House passed 70–22 (1 PNV) on 04/01/2024; transmitted to Governor 04/11/2024; "Signed by Governor" 04/17/2024, assigned Public Chapter 709; effective 07/01/2024. Prime sponsors Sen. Southerland / Rep. Fritts. — [TN General Assembly bill page](https://wapp.capitol.tn.gov/apps/BillInfo/Default.aspx?BillNumber=SB2691&GA=113)
- Operative text (Senate Amendment 1, SA0653, which replaced the bill): adds a new section to T.C.A. Title 68, Ch. 201, Part 1: "The intentional injection, release, or dispersion, by any means, of chemicals, chemical compounds, substances, or apparatus within the borders of this state into the atmosphere with the express purpose of affecting temperature, weather, or the intensity of the sunlight is prohibited." Effective July 1, 2024. **The act contains no penalty, enforcement, or definition section.** — [SA0653 amendment text (PDF)](https://www.capitol.tn.gov/Bills/113/Amend/SA0653.pdf)
- Scope: by its language ("affecting temperature, weather, or ... sunlight") it covers both cloud seeding and SRM/SAI; it does not use the words "cloud seeding" or "geoengineering" in the operative text (only in the preamble "WHEREAS" clauses). — [SA0653](https://www.capitol.tn.gov/Bills/113/Amend/SA0653.pdf)
- Date discrepancy: a third-party tracker lists "became law April 11, 2024"; the official page shows 04/11 as transmittal and 04/17 as signature. Use April 17, 2024 (or "April 2024"). — [FastDemocracy TN SB2691](https://fastdemocracy.com/bill/tn/113/bills/TNB00051591/) vs [official](https://wapp.capitol.tn.gov/apps/BillInfo/Default.aspx?BillNumber=SB2691&GA=113)

**Florida — SB 56 → Chapter 2025-157**
- Senate 28–9 on April 3, 2025; House 82–28 on April 30, 2025; signed by Gov. DeSantis June 20, 2025; chapter law 2025-157; effective July 1, 2025. Repeals weather-modification licensing (ss. 403.281–403.411); requires public-infrastructure operators (airports) to report monthly to FDOT. — [Florida Senate bill page](https://www.flsenate.gov/Session/Bill/2025/56)
- Penalties: third-degree felony; up to 5 years in prison and fine up to $100,000 for releasing substances to affect weather/temperature/sunlight; aircraft operators/controllers who fail reporting duties face up to 5 years and fine up to $5,000; DEP must create a reporting email/online form. — [News4Jax, June 20, 2025](https://www.news4jax.com/news/local/2025/06/20/gov-desantis-signs-bill-to-prohibit-activities-intended-to-affect-weather-temperature-sunlight-intensity/); [Gov. DeSantis press release](https://www.flgov.com/eog/news/press/2025/governor-ron-desantis-celebrates-action-protect-floridians-chemical-and)
- Scope: covers cloud seeding and SRM (prohibits acts "intended to affect the temperature, the weather, or the intensity of sunlight"); repeals the state's cloud-seeding permit program. — [Florida Senate bill page](https://www.flsenate.gov/Session/Bill/2025/56)
- Enforcement: AG Uthmeier's July 14, 2025 letter ordered all public airports to report weather-modification aircraft; monthly FDOT reports required from Oct. 1, 2025. — [CBS12, July 14, 2025](https://cbs12.com/news/local/florida-ag-enforces-new-law-banning-weather-modification-at-public-airports-james-uthmeier-altering-temperature-weather-climate-or-sunlight-intensity-south-florida-news-july-14-2025); [The Hill](https://thehill.com/homenews/state-watch/5401933-florida-weather-modification-geoengineering-law/); [Fox 35 Orlando](https://www.fox35orlando.com/news/florida-cracks-down-cloud-seeding-under-new-weather-modification-ban)
- Context: FL DEP records show no entity has applied for a weather-modification license since the requirement began in 1957. — [PolitiFact, July 21, 2025](https://www.politifact.com/article/2025/jul/21/florida-weather-modification-airports-geoengineeri/)

**Louisiana — SB 46 → Act No. 95 (2025)**
- Sen. Michael "Big Mike" Fesi. Senate passed 27–12 (04/28/2025); House 58–33 (05/29/2025); Senate concurred in House amendments 35–0 (06/01/2025); signed by Governor 06/08/2025 → Act No. 95; effective 08/01/2025. — [LA Legislature SB 46 bill info](https://legis.la.gov/legis/BillInfo.aspx?s=25RS&b=SB46&sbi=y)
- Résumé digest of Act 95: "prohibits the introduction of substances into the atmosphere for the express purpose of modifying weather, temperature, climate, or sunlight"; exceptions for firefighting, agriculture, and forestry under 1,000 ft AGL; anyone may report violations to LDEQ, which must set up intake procedures and publish reports; **repeals the prior weather-modification licensing law (R.S. 37:2201–2208)**, which had included penalties for modifying weather without a license; adds R.S. 30:2057(A)(3). **The digest lists no new civil or criminal penalty.** — [Act 95 Résumé Digest (PDF)](https://legis.la.gov/LEGIS/ViewDocument.aspx?d=1426146)
- Scope: both cloud seeding (it repeals the cloud-seeding license regime) and SRM.
- **Conflict on penalties:** one aggregator claims "up to $200,000 per violation"; Newsweek's House-stage coverage says lawmakers removed penalties in favor of reporting/investigation. The official digest supports the latter (no penalty section). — [Newsweek, Louisiana passes bill](https://www.newsweek.com/louisiana-pill-ban-chemtrails-2079764); [Fox 8, May 30, 2025](https://www.fox8live.com/2025/05/30/louisiana-lawmakers-push-chemtrail-ban-legislation-through-house/)

**Louisiana — SB 189 → Act No. 601 (2026) — NEW since the site was last updated**
- Sen. Fesi. Prefiled 02/26/2026; Senate Environmental Quality reported favorably 04/14; Senate passed 29–2 on 04/20/2026; House Natural Resources & Environment reported 10–0 on 05/12; House final passage 74–22 on 05/19/2026 without amendment; sent to Governor 05/21; **"Became Act No. 601 without the Governor's signature" 06/01/2026; effective 08/01/2026.** — [LA Legislature SB 189 bill info](https://legis.la.gov/legis/BillInfo.aspx?s=26RS&b=SB189&sbi=y)
- Substance: a one-line amendment to the 2025 prohibition adding "including by the burning of fuel in an aircraft engine" to the list of prohibited methods of dispersal; GeoLawWatch characterizes it as declaratory/political rather than a substantive expansion. — [GeoLawWatch LA SB189](https://www.geolawwatch.com/bill/2125167)
- No news coverage of the signing/Act number was found; the only source is the official bill page (searched Oct 26, 2026).

**No other enactments.** Searches for 2026 signings in Iowa, Oklahoma, Texas, Arizona, Arkansas, Kentucky, Missouri, Alabama, Georgia, South Carolina, West Virginia, Ohio, Idaho, Wyoming, Utah, New Hampshire, Mississippi returned none; GeoLawWatch's 2026-season tally lists "Passed: 3 (Idaho, Louisiana, New Hampshire)" but the Idaho and New Hampshire items are non-binding memorials/resolutions, not statutes (see KQ4). — [GeoLawWatch tracker, updated Oct 20, 2026](https://www.geolawwatch.com/)

### Inferences
- The enacted count the site should publish is "3 states (TN, FL, LA); Louisiana strengthened its law in 2026." Counting Act 601 as a "fourth ban" would be wrong — it is an amendment in the same state.
- Tennessee's and Louisiana's laws have no stated penalty; only Florida's is a felony. The site's phrasing should not imply all three are criminal bans.
- Louisiana's prohibition sits inside the Environmental Quality Act (R.S. 30:2057), so LDEQ's general air-quality enforcement powers may apply — but no source confirms this; treat as unverified.

### Gaps
- Could not retrieve the enrolled text of LA Act 601 itself (only the bill-history page and GeoLawWatch summary).
- Could not retrieve the full enrolled text of TN PC 709 from the Secretary of State site (403); relied on the Senate amendment that became the law.

## Key Question 6: Which states run active cloud-seeding programs in 2026; did any halt?

### Takeaway
The "9 states" figure holds (GAO, Dec 2024: California, Colorado, Idaho, Nevada, New Mexico, North Dakota, Texas, Utah, Wyoming). No source reports a state halting its program in 2026; Texas's July 2025 pause after the Hill Country floods was lifted, and Idaho's bill to repeal cloud seeding died.

### Cited Findings
- GAO (published Dec 19, 2024): "Nine U.S. states are currently using it"; most common uses are precipitation enhancement and hail suppression via silver iodide; federal involvement limited. — [GAO-25-107328](https://www.gao.gov/products/gao-25-107328)
- The nine states, per summaries citing GAO: California, Colorado, Idaho, Nevada, New Mexico, North Dakota, Texas, Utah, Wyoming. — [Factually (citing GAO)](https://factually.co/fact-checks/environment/which-us-states-conduct-cloud-seeding-programs-oversight-7833fb); [Scientific American "Eight states"](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/eight-states-are-seeding-clouds-to-overcome-megadrought/)
- Utah (2026 update): state "continues to support operational cloud seeding activities"; ground-based program now fully remote; exploring drones to replace aircraft tested 2022–2025. — [Utah DWR 2026 updates](https://water.utah.gov/?p=1266); [Utah DWR cloud seeding](https://water.utah.gov/cloudseeding/)
- Nevada: SB 6 (2025) funds DRI's state program for the 2025–27 biennium. — [The New American](https://thenewamerican.com/us/environment/states-push-back-against-geoengineering-and-cloud-seeding/); DRI current operations list CO and CA projects. — [DRI](https://www.dri.edu/cloud-seeding-program/current-cloud-seeding-operations/)
- Idaho: IWRB cloud seeding program page lists seasonal basins; H0669 (2026), which would have repealed Idaho's cloud-seeding law, died. — [IDWR Cloud Seeding Program](https://idwr.idaho.gov/iwrb-overview/iwrb-programs-overview/cloud-seeding-program/); [BillTrack50 ID H0669](https://www.billtrack50.com/billdetail/1972531)
- Texas: all seeding paused after the July 4, 2025 floods (29 flights before suspension July 2); operations later resumed; TDLR found the July 2, 2025 Karnes County operation did not contribute to the flooding. On Aug 27, 2026 the TDLR Weather Modification Advisory Board voted to propose a rule requiring operators to file operation reports quarterly with TDLR (not just on their own sites) — proposal only, not yet adopted. — [KXAN resumption](https://www.kxan.com/weather/weather-modification-operations-resume-after-suspension-following-july-4-floods/); [Agri-Pulse suspension](https://www.agri-pulse.com/articles/23207-texas-suspends-cloud-seeding-amid-floods-speculation); [Texas Dispatch, Aug 29, 2026](https://thetexasdispatch.com/tdlr-weather-modification-advisory-board-august-27-2026-operation-reports-rule/)
- Tennessee, Florida, and Louisiana repealed their (unused) weather-modification licensing statutes via their bans (see KQ1). — [Act 95 digest](https://legis.la.gov/LEGIS/ViewDocument.aspx?d=1426146); [FL SB 56](https://www.flsenate.gov/Session/Bill/2025/56)
- Federal pressure: HHS Secretary RFK Jr.'s October 2026 comments on "chemtrails" and weather modification drew pushback from cloud-seeding states such as Utah. — [Washington Sun / NOTUS](https://www.washingtonsun.com/climate-environment/rfk-maha-cloud-seeding-weather-modification-utah?redirected=notus_org); [Integrity Magazine, Oct 7, 2026](https://integritymagazine.org/2026/10/07/robert-f-kennedy-jr-s-chemtrail-comments-revive-geoengineering-controversy/)

### Inferences
- The site's "9 states" claim is still accurate as of the latest official inventory (GAO, Dec 2024); no 2026 official re-count exists, so cite GAO with its date.

### Gaps
- No 2026-season flight/operations statistics for Texas, Idaho, Wyoming, North Dakota, or New Mexico were found; TDLR and state water boards would be the primary sources.
